## Overview

**Drupal** is a content management system (CMS) used to build many of the websites and applications people use every day. Key highlights:

### What Makes Drupal Special
- **Great standard features**: Easy content authoring, reliable performance, excellent security
- **Flexibility &amp; Modularity**: Core design principle allowing versatile, structured content
- **Extensibility**: Thousands of add-ons available:
  - **Modules** – expand functionality
  - **Themes** – customize presentation
  - **Distributions** – packaged starter-kits

### Open Source &amp; Free
- Distributed under the **GNU General Public License (GPL)**
- **No licensing fees, ever** — Drupal will always be free
- Anyone can download, use, modify, and share it

### Global Community
- One of the largest open-source communities in the world
- **1,000,000+** passionate developers, designers, trainers, strategists, coordinators, editors, and sponsors

### Notable Drupal Users
- **Government**: U.S. federal agencies, London, France
- **Media**: BBC, NBC, MTV UK
- **Nonprofits/Education**: Amnesty International, University of Oxford
