# About Drupal — Summary

Here's an overview of the "About" page from Drupal.org:

## What is Drupal?
Drupal is **content management software** used to build many websites and applications you use every day. Key characteristics:

- **Standard features**: Easy content authoring, reliable performance, and excellent security
- **Flexibility**: Modularity is a core principle, allowing versatile, structured content
- **Extensibility**: Thousands of add-ons available, including:
  - **Modules** – expand Drupal's functionality
  - **Themes** – customize content presentation
  - **Distributions** – packaged Drupal bundles for use as starter-kits

## Open Source &amp; Licensing
- Drupal is **open source software** — anyone can download, use, modify, and share it
- Built on principles of **collaboration, globalism, and innovation**
- Distributed under the **GNU General Public License (GPL)**
- **No licensing fees, ever** — Drupal will always be free

## The Community
- One of the **largest open source communities** in the world
- **1,000,000+** passionate developers, designers, trainers, strategists, coordinators, editors, and sponsors

## Who Uses Drupal
Notable organizations using Drupal include:
- **Government**: U.S. government agencies, London, France
- **Media**: BBC, NBC, MTV UK
- **Nonprofits/Education**: Amnesty International, University of Oxford

## Recent News (from the page)
- Drupal 11.4.0 is now available
- Drupal CMS leadership changes
- New Site Templates launched
- "Drupal at 25: Built to Last, Ready for What's Next"
- Drupal's AI Roadmap for 2026

## Getting Started
The page points users to:
- **Download Drupal core** or a pre-built distribution
- Explore **modules, themes, and translations**
- Find **Drupal experts/agencies** via the services marketplace
- Explore **Drupal Jobs** for hiring or job-seeking

## Ownership
Drupal is a **registered trademark of Dries Buytaert**, the original creator of Drupal.
