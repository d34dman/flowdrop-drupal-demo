# Drupal.org "About" Page Summary

Here's the content from the About page at drupal.org:

## What is Drupal?

**Drupal is content management software** used to build many of the websites and applications people use daily. Key characteristics:

- **Flexibility &amp; Modularity** — one of its core design principles
- **Great standard features**: easy content authoring, reliable performance, excellent security
- **Extensible** via thousands of add-ons:
  - **Modules** — expand functionality
  - **Themes** — customize presentation
  - **Distributions** — pre-packaged starter-kits

### Open Source &amp; Free
- Licensed under the **GNU General Public License (GPL)**
- **No licensing fees, ever** — Drupal will always be free
- Anyone can download, use, modify, and share it

## The Community

- One of the **largest open source communities** in the world
- **1,000,000+** developers, designers, trainers, strategists, coordinators, editors, and sponsors

## Who Uses Drupal

Notable users include:
- **Government**: U.S. agencies, City of London, French Government
- **Media**: BBC, NBC, MTV UK
- **Nonprofits/Education**: Amnesty International, University of Oxford
