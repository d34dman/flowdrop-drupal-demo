## What is Drupal?
Drupal is **open-source content management software** used to build many websites and applications. Key highlights:

- **Flexibility &amp; Modularity** — one of Drupal's core design principles, letting you build versatile, structured content for dynamic web experiences
- **Extensible** — thousands of add-ons available:
  - **Modules** – expand functionality
  - **Themes** – customize presentation
  - **Distributions** – pre-packaged starter-kit bundles
- **Free forever** — licensed under the **GNU General Public License (GPL)**, with no licensing fees ever

## Community
The Drupal community is one of the **largest open-source communities in the world**, with over **1,000,000** contributors — developers, designers, trainers, strategists, coordinators, editors, and sponsors — collaboratively building the project, offering support, writing documentation, and organizing events.

## Notable Users
Drupal powers sites for organizations including:
- **Governments** — U.S. federal agencies, London, France
- **Media** — BBC, NBC, MTV UK
- **Nonprofits/Education** — Amnesty International, University of Oxford

## Current Version
**Drupal 11** is the latest release, focused on improving structured content, workflows, and content governance for site builders.
