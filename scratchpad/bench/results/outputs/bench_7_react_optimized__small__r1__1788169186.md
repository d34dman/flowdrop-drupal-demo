# About Drupal

Here's a summary of the content from **drupal.org/about**:

## What is Drupal?

Drupal is **content management software** used to build many websites and applications used every day. Key characteristics:

- **Great standard features**: easy content authoring, reliable performance, excellent security
- **Highly flexible &amp; modular**: this is one of its core design principles
- **Extensible**: thousands of add-ons available, including:
  - **Modules** – expand Drupal's functionality
  - **Themes** – customize content presentation
  - **Distributions** – pre-packaged Drupal bundles (starter-kits)

Drupal can also integrate with external services and other applications in your infrastructure, making it powerful for building integrated digital frameworks.

## Open Source &amp; Free

- Drupal is **open source software** — anyone can download, use, modify, and share it
- Built on principles of **collaboration, globalism, and innovation**
- Distributed under the **GNU General Public License (GPL)**
- **No licensing fees, ever** — Drupal will always be free

## Global Community

The Drupal community is one of the **largest open source communities in the world**, with more than **1,000,000** passionate developers, designers, trainers, strategists, coordinators, editors, and sponsors working together.

## Who Uses Drupal

Drupal powers websites for a wide range of prominent organizations, including:
- **Government agencies** (U.S. government, London, France)
- **Media companies** (BBC, NBC, MTV UK)
- **Nonprofits/Universities** (Amnesty International, University of Oxford)

## Drupal 11

The latest major version, **Drupal 11**, continues to enhance the platform's strengths — making structured content, workflows, and content governance more flexible for ambitious builders.
