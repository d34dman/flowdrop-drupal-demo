    <div class="field field-name-body field-type-text-with-summary field-label-hidden"><div class="field-items"><div class="field-item even"><h2>The digital experiences you love. The organizations you trust most. The software they depend on.</h2>
<h3>Make something amazing, for anyone</h3>
<p>Drupal is content management software. It's used to make many of the websites and applications you use every day. Drupal has great standard features, like easy content authoring, reliable performance, and excellent security. But what sets it apart is its flexibility; modularity is one of its core principles. Its tools help you build the versatile, structured content that dynamic web experiences need.</p>
<p>It's also a great choice for creating integrated digital frameworks. You can extend it with any one, or many, of thousands of add-ons. Modules expand Drupal's functionality. Themes let you customize your content's presentation. Distributions are packaged Drupal bundles you can use as starter-kits. Mix and match these components to enhance Drupal's core abilities. Or, integrate Drupal with external services and other applications in your infrastructure. No other content management software is this powerful and scalable.</p>
<p>The Drupal project is open source software. Anyone can download, use, work on, and share it with others. It's built on principles like collaboration, globalism, and innovation. It's distributed under the terms of the <a target="_blank">GNU General Public License</a> (GPL). There are <a href="/about/licensing" title="FAQ about Drupal licensing">no licensing fees</a>, ever. Drupal will always be free.</p>
<div class="click-to-tweet-box">
<h3><a target="_blank">Drupal's the content management system behind many of the digital experiences you love. And it will always be free.</a></h3>
<p><a target="_blank">Tweet</a></p>
</div>

<h3>Made possible by a global community</h3>
<p>The <a href="/community" title="Drupal community landing page">Drupal community</a> is one of the largest open source communities in the world. We're more than 1,000,000 passionate developers, designers, trainers, strategists, coordinators, editors, and sponsors working together. We build Drupal, provide support, create documentation, share networking opportunities, and more. Our shared commitment to the open source spirit pushes the Drupal project forward.</p>
<!-- Start top 2 col grid row --><div class="about-grid-4">
<h3><a href="/about/11">Drupal 11</a></h3>
<p>Drupal 11 continues enhancing the strengths of the platform. It makes structured content, workflows, and content governance more flexible and easier for ambitious builders.<br>
<a href="/about/10">Build the best of the web with Drupal 11</a>.</p>
</div>
<div class="about-grid-4 omega">
<h3><a href="/case-studies">Who uses Drupal</a></h3>
<p>Drupal is the platform many government agencies in <a target="_blank" title="WhiteHouse.gov">the United States</a>, <a target="_blank">London</a>, <a target="_blank">France</a>, and more use to communicate with citizens. It's the framework media companies like <a target="_blank">BBC</a>, <a target="_blank">NBC</a>, and <a target="_blank">MTV UK</a> rely on to inform and entertain the world. It's part of how organizations and universities like <a target="_blank">Amnesty International</a> and the <a target="_blank">University of Oxford</a> work to make the world a better place. Read <a>Drupal case studies</a> or check out a list of <a href="/organizations">organizations with profiles on Drupal.org</a>.</p>
</div>
<!-- End top 2 col grid --><!-- Start mid 2 col grid --><div class="about-grid-4">
<h3><a href="/start">Get started</a></h3>
<p>Ready to build something amazing? <a href="start">Get started</a> with an overview of working with Drupal and read about its technical requirements. Next, check out a pre-built <a href="/project/installation+profiles">Drupal distribution</a> or <a href="/download">download Drupal core</a>. You can then expand and enhance your work with <a href="/project/modules">modules</a>, <a href="/project/themes">themes</a>, and <a>translations</a>. The possibilities are endless.</p>
</div>
<div class="about-grid-4 omega">
<h3><a href="/marketplace" title="Drupal services marketplace">Find Drupal experts</a></h3>
<p>There are lots of <a href="/marketplace" title="Drupal services marketplace">companies who'd love to help with your Drupal project</a>. They're experts in design, development, <a href="/hosting">hosting</a>, spam blocking, theming, <a href="/training-services">training</a>, and more. If, instead, you're looking for a new employee or freelancer, try the best place for hiring great Drupal talent: <a target="_blank">Drupal Jobs</a>.</p>
