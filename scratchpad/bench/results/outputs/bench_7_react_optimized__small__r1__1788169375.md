# About Drupal.org

Here's a summary of the content from the Drupal.org About page:

## Overview

**Drupal** is content management software used to build many of the websites and applications people use every day. It's known for:
- Easy content authoring
- Reliable performance
- Excellent security
- **Flexibility &amp; modularity** (its core distinguishing principle)

## Key Features

- **Extensible architecture**: Thousands of add-ons available including:
  - **Modules** – expand Drupal's functionality
  - **Themes** – customize content presentation
  - **Distributions** – pre-packaged Drupal bundles as starter-kits

- **Open Source**: Distributed under the **GNU General Public License (GPL)**
  - Anyone can download, use, modify, and share it
  - **No licensing fees, ever**
  - Built on principles of collaboration, globalism, and innovation

## The Community

- One of the **largest open source communities** in the world
- **1,000,000+** passionate contributors: developers, designers, trainers, strategists, coordinators, editors, and sponsors

## Who Uses Drupal

Notable organizations using Drupal include:
- **Government**: Various US government agencies, London, France
- **Media**: BBC, NBC, MTV UK
- **Nonprofits/Education**: Amnesty International, University of Oxford
