# About Drupal.org — Summary

Here's the content from the Drupal.org "About" page:

## What is Drupal?

Drupal is **content management software** used to build many of the websites and applications people use every day. Key highlights:

- **Standard features**: easy content authoring, reliable performance, excellent security
- **Flexibility &amp; modularity**: a core design principle
- **Extensibility**: thousands of add-ons available including:
  - **Modules** – expand functionality
  - **Themes** – customize presentation
  - **Distributions** – packaged starter-kits
- Can integrate with external services and other applications

## Open Source Foundation

- Drupal is **open source software** — anyone can download, use, modify, and share it
- Built on principles of **collaboration, globalism, and innovation**
- Licensed under the **GNU General Public License (GPL)**
- **No licensing fees, ever** — Drupal will always be free

## The Community

- One of the **largest open source communities** in the world
- More than **1,000,000** developers, designers, trainers, strategists, coordinators, editors, and sponsors
- Collectively builds Drupal, provides support, creates documentation, and organizes events

## Notable Users
Drupal powers sites for:
- **Government**: White House (via US agencies), London, France
- **Media**: BBC, NBC, MTV UK
- **Nonprofits/Education**: Amnesty International, University of Oxford
